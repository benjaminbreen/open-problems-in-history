Archivists and librarians

cataloguing, digitization, locating records

Many of these problems are stalled not for lack of ideas but because the records that would settle them are uncatalogued, undigitized, or scattered across institutions. Archivists and librarians know where those records are, what condition they are in, and what it would take to make them searchable.

  1. How, if at all, did Copernicus learn of the Maragha-school planetary models?

    Examine ownership notes, bindings and library inventories of Greek and Hebrew astronomical miscellanies in Italian, Polish and Central European collections, including the Jagiellonian Library.

    Science & knowledge · Europe · 1250–1543
  2. Which lost works of Galen survive unrecognised in Arabic and Syriac?

    Identify unedited Galenic translations listed by Sezgin, Ullmann and Fichtner in the Süleymaniye, Iranian libraries and the Escorial, and arrange imaging and catalogue records.

    Texts & authorship · Middle East · 160–1270
  3. By what route did knowledge of gunpowder reach Latin Europe in the thirteenth century?

    Locate, describe and date the earliest Liber ignium, Feuerwerkbuch and Arabic furūsiyya witnesses in Paris, Munich, Leeds, Istanbul and Cairo, and report which remain undigitized.

    Science & knowledge · Global · 1040–1350
  4. How many people were carried in the Indian Ocean slave trades, 1500–1850?

    Flag mentions of enslaved people in the GLOBALISE transcriptions of the VOC Overgekomen Brieven en Papieren and the Cape Slave Office deeds, and draft source-linked transfer records.

    Economy · Indian Ocean · 1500–1850
  5. How many Indigenous people were enslaved in the Americas between 1492 and 1900?

    Transcribe and flag candidate entries in the Escribanos de Santiago and Real Audiencia records for 1608–1683, linking the same person across sales, baptisms, lawsuits and inventories.

    Demography · Americas · 1492–1900
  6. How many people were killed in the Irish rising of 1641?

    Code each deposition in TCD MS 836 for reported killings, recording victim, place, date, manner and whether the deponent was an eyewitness or repeating hearsay.

    Demography · Europe · 1641–1642
  7. Did Kerala-school infinite series reach early modern Europe through Jesuit missionaries?

    Read the Documenta Indica and the ARSI Goa-Malabar letters for references to Indian astronomy, Brahmin informants and mathematical manuscripts, and register each mention by Jesuit, place and date.

    Science & knowledge · South Asia · 1350–1700
  8. How many early printed editions have been lost without trace?

    Reconstruct earlier states of ISTC and GW from published versions and dated records, and build one record per edition with surviving copy count, format, language and sheet count.

    Texts & authorship · Europe · 1450–1600
  9. Can the Meroitic language be translated beyond its funerary formulae?

    Turn the scanned REM volumes on Persée and inscriptions published since 2000 into a lemmatized, machine-readable corpus, recording which editor made each word segmentation.

    Languages & scripts · Africa · 300 BCE–450 CE
  10. Did the population of Ottoman Anatolia collapse in the late sixteenth and seventeenth centuries?

    Locate and transcribe tahrir, avarız and cizye registers for the same districts in the TT.d, MAD.d and KK.d series, tracing every figure to a register page.

    Demography · Middle East · 1520–1700
  11. Why did plague disappear from western Europe after the early eighteenth century?

    Digitize and extract ship arrivals, bills of health and quarantine durations from the health office archives of Marseille, Venice, Livorno, Genoa, Dubrovnik and Malta.

    Disease · Europe · 1650–1850
  12. How literate was the population of late imperial China?

    Locate signature blocks in the Huizhou, Qingshui River and Taiwan deed facsimile series and record each party's form of subscription and the named scribe.

    Texts & authorship · East Asia · 1644–1911
  13. Were Moses Shapira's Deuteronomy fragments an ancient text or an 1880s forgery?

    Locate and describe every surviving witness, from British Library Add MS 41294 and the Berlin Shapira inventory to the 1883 lithographs, so they can be aligned as variants.

    Texts & authorship · Middle East · 1878–1885
  14. How much American silver reached China between 1550 and 1800?

    Read the Manila treasury accounts and galleon registers in the AGI against the Ramo Filipinas in the AGN to produce a reconciled Acapulco-Manila series for 1571 to 1815.

    Economy · Global · 1550–1800
  15. Which volcanoes caused the great unattributed sulfate spikes in polar ice, above all 1809?

    Trace each claimed eyewitness report of ashfall, dimmed sun or red twilights back to its original text and edition, recording exact wording, place and date.

    Places · Global · 1229–1810
  16. What do James Wilkinson's unread coded dispatches to Spanish officials say?

    Locate the Library of Congress photostats of AGI Papeles de Cuba legajos 2373 to 2375 and compare them with the originals in Seville.

    Texts & authorship · Americas · 1787–1807
