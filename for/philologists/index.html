Philologists and epigraphers

historical languages, scripts and manuscripts: medieval Arabic and Persian, Classical Chinese, Sanskrit, Ge'ez, cuneiform, Meroitic and others

Much of the evidence lies in manuscripts, inscriptions and early printed texts whose languages, scripts and conventions take specialist training to read: medieval Arabic and Persian manuscripts, Classical Chinese, Sanskrit, Ge'ez, cuneiform, Meroitic and more. Philologists and epigraphers are needed to read, edit and check these sources, including whatever AI tools extract from them.

12 problems need philologists and epigraphers

  1. 01
    When was Beowulf composed?

    Annotate Old English poems and dated prose for metrical, morphological and orthographic features, marking which are authorial and which scribal, using the Dictionary of Old English Corpus.

    Chronology · Europe · 680–1020also needs computational linguists
  2. 02
    How, if at all, did Copernicus learn of the Maragha-school planetary models?

    Census every manuscript carrying the Ṭūsī couple or an Ibn al-Shāṭir-type model in any language, recording which device variant each witness carries and its date.

    Science & knowledge · Europe · 1250–1543also needs archivists
  3. 03
    Which lost works of Galen survive unrecognised in Arabic and Syriac?

    Match Galen-attributed passages in al-Hawi, Ibn Abi Usaybi'a and Maimonides to works lost in Greek, using Galen's and Hunayn's descriptions of their contents.

    Texts & authorship · Middle East · 160–1270also needs archivists and computational linguists
  4. 04
    By what route did knowledge of gunpowder reach Latin Europe in the thirteenth century?

    Transcribe and collate dated gunpowder and saltpetre recipes in Chinese, Arabic, Persian, Hebrew and Latin, recording the attested senses of terms such as bārūd and sal petrosum.

    Science & knowledge · Global · 1040–1350also needs archivists
  5. 05
    Did anyone write down the Aceh tsunamis of c. 1394 and c. 1450?

    Search the Ming Shilu, Ma Huan, Fei Xin, Gong Zhen and the Malay chronicles for sea floods or abandoned towns, and record every passage and edition examined.

    Chronology · Indian Ocean · 1390–1460also needs geoscientists
  6. 06
    Did Kerala-school infinite series reach early modern Europe through Jesuit missionaries?

    Survey Kerala astronomical manuscripts at the Oriental Research Institute and the Royal Asiatic Society for ownership marks, copying notes or Latin and Portuguese annotations showing European hands.

    Science & knowledge · South Asia · 1350–1700also needs archivists
  7. 07
    What language does Linear A record, and can any proposed family beat chance?

    Build a single concordance of GORILA, its 2025 supplement, SigLA and lineara.xyz, encoding Linear B sound values in tiers from palaeographically secure to assumed.

    Languages & scripts · Mediterranean · 1800–1450 BCEalso needs computational linguists
  8. 08
    Can the Meroitic language be translated beyond its funerary formulae?

    Compile a graded glossary of recurring words outside the funerary formulae, separating secure, probable and conjectural meanings, and test translation hypotheses drawn from Old Nubian against the Hamadab stela.

    Languages & scripts · Africa · 300 BCE–450 CEalso needs archaeologists and archivists
  9. 09
    Does Proto-Elamite record language, and is it the ancestor of Linear Elamite?

    Test the claimed relation to Linear Elamite with pre-registered sign-form and value correspondences scored against shuffled pairings, using the CDLI transliterations and images.

    Languages & scripts · Middle East · 3300–1880 BCEalso needs computational linguists
  10. 10
    When was the consonantal text of the Qurʾān standardized?

    Align early manuscript transcriptions from Corpus Coranicum verse by verse, coding each difference as orthographic, consonantal or verse division, and test single-archetype and fluid-text stemmata.

    Texts & authorship · Middle East · 632–750also needs archaeologists and conservation scientists
  11. 11
    Were Moses Shapira's Deuteronomy fragments an ancient text or an 1880s forgery?

    Classify every orthographic, palaeographic and textual feature of the transcriptions as attested in sources available by 1883 or only in later material such as Qumran Deuteronomy manuscripts.

    Texts & authorship · Middle East · 1878–1885also needs archivists and conservation scientists
  12. 12
    In what year did the Zhou conquer the Shang?

    Compile the Western Zhou bronze date formulas into a machine-readable corpus, and set out where the current and ancient Bamboo Annals texts disagree on reign counts.

    Chronology · East Asia · 1130–1018 BCEalso needs astronomers and archaeologists
