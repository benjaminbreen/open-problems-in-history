    The day the sea ran up the rivers

    In 1738, people on an Indonesian island fled uphill as seawater rushed inland. A Dutch official described it twice. His letters preserve evidence of a tsunami missing from modern catalogues.

  2. Pachacámac, Peru · Andean records

    Did two sets of knotted cords keep the same account?

    People in the Andes kept records by tying knots in cords. Two objects from the coast of Peru preserve a run of numbers in the same order. Even one cord that broke long ago offers a clue.

  3. Mauritius, 1615 · Natural history

    The dodos in a ship’s journal

    In April 1615, Dutch sailors went ashore on Mauritius and brought dodos back to their ship. Two entries in their journal fill a gap in the short written history of this extinct, flightless bird.

    In 1902 the astronomer Samuel Langley asked Alexander Graham Bell to help him fly an experimental kite. A notebook records two changes Bell suggested. Did either design get into the air?
