Research stub · Mughal India · Seventeenth century

How did a dodo become the subject of a Mughal painting?

A painter at the Mughal court depicted a bird native to Mauritius. The traveller Peter Mundy recalled seeing two live dodos in Surat, a port on India’s west coast. Can a particular bird be traced to the artist?

A ship’s cargo list or a letter about a gift could connect the arrival of a live bird in India with someone at court. The album catalogue dates the study to about 1615. Any proposed journey would have to fit that date.
