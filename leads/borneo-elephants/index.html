How did wild elephants come to live on Borneo?

One account traces Borneo’s wild elephants to a royal gift. Elephants from Java supposedly reached Borneo by way of the Sulu islands. If those animals founded the herd, some of Java’s vanished elephant population may have living descendants.

A 2018 genetic study favored ancient settlement on Borneo. A 2025 study found evidence consistent with a much more recent introduction. Dutch records mention elephants at Javanese courts, including animals imported from elsewhere. A letter naming a donor and recipient could document a journey. To connect it to today’s herd, we would also need evidence of where those elephants came from and what happened after they arrived.

  • The historical case for a Javan origin, Sarawak Museum Journal (2007)
  • Sharma and colleagues’ genetic study (2018)
  • Kappelhof and colleagues’ genome study (2025)
  • Dutch East India Company records at GLOBALISE
  • Published genealogies and documents in The History of Sulu
