Where did Melbourne’s toromiro trees come from?

The toromiro, a tree native to Rapa Nui (Easter Island), survives in gardens. Melbourne’s trees might preserve a separate line of descent from the island’s wild trees. A pressed specimen offers a place to begin.

The label on a specimen from garden bed FE82 points to an earlier specimen taken from the same tree. Matching those two records to Melbourne’s planting register might identify the donor and show whether the tree came through Gothenburg, a known source of cultivated toromiro.

  • Specimen CHR 524208, Allan Herbarium
  • Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria’s living collection
  • Heenan and colleagues’ genetic study of cultivated toromiro
