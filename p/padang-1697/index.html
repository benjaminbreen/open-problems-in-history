Which earthquake sent waves up the Padang River in 1697?

A Dutch dispatch describes ten or more surges after the Padang earthquake. Can the waves be traced to a rupture offshore?

Science & knowledge · Indian Ocean · 1697–1703sciencedigitization
The day the sea ran up the rivers →Did anyone write down the Aceh tsunamis of c. 1394 and c. 1450? →

Why it matters

The Padang council describes surges that broke vessels from their moorings after the earthquake of 23 February 1697. A study of Mentawai corals had considered 1697 for an uplift dated around 1703 and inferred limited mainland effects from the available accounts.

Why it is open

The dispatch gives a time and several places, with different levels of certainty. It gives no dependable wave heights, and the coral date allows more than one candidate earthquake.

What would count as a solution

Independent, dated observations of the waves at several coastal sites could constrain a source, especially if the resulting rupture model also fits the coral evidence.

Potential approach

  • Find the enclosed damage survey named in the council dispatch and read the April and May York Fort letters received at Madras in July. Compare the locations and timing with models of offshore ruptures.
