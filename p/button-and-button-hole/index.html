Who printed Button, and Button-Hole in 1723?

The bawdy pamphlet names the fictitious A. Moore. Several of its ornaments also appear in books naming Thomas Edlin as printer.

Texts & authorship · Europe · 1715–1735forensics
Who printed the anonymous and false-imprint books of eighteenth-century Britain? →

Why it matters

A convincing match would help place an anonymous pamphlet inside London’s printing trade. The case tests how much ownership and workshop practice can be inferred from repeated ornaments.

Why it is open

A false imprint conceals the press. Similar-looking impressions can come from separate blocks, and printers could buy, borrow or inherit blocks.

What would count as a solution

Identify shared wear on several blocks and show that the dated comparison impressions fit one workshop at the time of printing.

Potential approach

  • Align scans of multiple copies and compare chips, cuts and wear. Look for the same blocks in contemporary publications associated with Edlin’s competitors as a control.
