Which cultivated toromiro trees descend from wild Rapa Nui collections?

Melbourne’s toromiro trees may preserve an early introduction of a species extinct in the wild. Can their garden records resolve the pedigree?

Why it matters

An independently documented wild collection would clarify how much of the original population persists in gardens. That could guide comparisons among living plants used in conservation.

Why it is open

Melbourne’s flora describes material from Gothenburg in the 1970s. Genetic studies leave the origin of the sampled Melbourne plants open. One voucher points to an earlier sheet from the same tree.

What would count as a solution

Tie the sampled tree in bed FE82 to its accession and donor. A second, undated Melbourne specimen filed as kōwhai would also need a reliable date and botanical redetermination.

Potential approach

  • Start with CHR 524208 and its reference to the earlier MEL sheet. Compare the garden’s bed inventory and accession records with documented Gothenburg transfers.
