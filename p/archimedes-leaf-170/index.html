Where is leaf 170 of the Archimedes Palimpsest?

A 1906 photograph identifies the missing leaf. Where did it go, and what readings might the physical page still preserve?

Texts & authorship · Europe · 1906–2026digitizationscience

Why it matters

Locating the leaf could recover text or correct readings obscured in the old photograph. The case also offers a way to recognize a dispersed manuscript leaf even if it has acquired a different description.

Why it is open

The photograph shows wording, ruling and damage. An auction entry or catalogue may show only one side or describe the leaf under its later prayer text.

What would count as a solution

Match an independently located leaf to the photograph through the placement of text, ruling and defects, then verify and publish its provenance.

Potential approach

  • Trace former owner Anne Guersan’s correspondence and compare candidate leaves with the 1906 image, allowing for rotations, later additions and cropping.
