How did a dodo become the subject of a Mughal painting?

A Mughal bird study apparently depicts a dodo. Can the bird that served as its model be traced from Mauritius to India?

Material & visual · South Asia · 1600–1640digitization

Why it matters

A connection between the study and a documented bird would show how a living animal moved from Mauritius into a South Asian world of trade, gifts and painting.

Why it is open

Peter recalled seeing two living dodos in Surat. The album catalogue dates the bird study to about 1615, attributes it to Manṣūr, and describes the assembled page as a later collage. No surviving record connects a particular bird to the artist.

What would count as a solution

A dated shipping or gift record should link a landing in India to a person or place connected with the painter, with a chronology that fits the study.

Potential approach

  • Trace animals through original cargo lists, letters and gifts. Use the published 1615 Wapen van Amsterdam journal as a starting point and test the attribution and dating independently.
